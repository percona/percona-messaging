# Partners

Percona helps partners cut customer database cost, keep customers free of vendor lock-in, and run open source databases on-prem, in hybrid estates, or across clouds.

## How we partner

Percona works with companies that help customers decide three things: what databases should cost, who controls them, and where they run. The partner brings the customer relationship. Percona brings database software and engineers who run those engines in production.

Most work starts with consulting, professional services, or migration help. If the customer later needs day-to-day coverage (backups, high availability, upgrades, or on-call), the partnership can grow into longer operational support.

How the deal is structured depends on the partner type. Below, each type pairs **who** with **how we work together**.

### Global systems integrators (GSI / SI)

**Who:** Large integrators that serve enterprise and global accounts (for example Infosys, CGI, Cognizant, Slalom, Wipro, Ahead).

**How we work together**

- Main models: **sell-with** (joint pursuit) and **sell-through** (the GSI sells Percona offers as part of its own portfolio)
- Not the usual path: referral-only deals
- Percona offers can sit on the GSI’s existing menu, so big accounts often skip a new RFP for each database need
- We agree a **floor price** with the GSI. The GSI marks it up and sells to its customer
- When a project needs Percona engineers, that work runs under a statement of work on the sell-through path
- On very large accounts, some GSIs also use **revenue share**

**Customer gets:** One integrator relationship, a ready menu of database software and services, and known pricing.

### Independent software vendors (ISV)

**Who:** Software companies that pair their product with Percona database software and expertise (for example HexaCluster, Red Hat, SUSE, Solanica, SIOS).

**How we work together**

- Main models: **reseller**, **sell-with**, and **sell-through**
- We do not always sell *to* the ISV. That happens mainly when the ISV needs Percona services for its own systems
- More often we go to market together so the customer buys a fuller stack (platform plus open source or source-available database software and expertise) under clear commercial terms

**Customer gets:** Fewer vendors and contracts, and a fuller stack from one motion.

### Hyperscalers

**Who:** Major public clouds where customers already buy (AWS, GCP).

**How we work together**

- Deals follow the hyperscaler’s **marketplace** and partner contract forms
- Pricing and packaging have little room to change compared with other partner types

**Customer gets:** Percona-supported databases inside a cloud they already know how to buy from (listed offers, familiar procurement).

### Cloud providers

**Who:** Cloud companies that put Percona expertise inside their own database services. **DigitalOcean** is the live example today.

**How we work together**

- Model: **one-to-many**. Percona supplies engineering expertise. The cloud provider builds that into its own MySQL and PostgreSQL offerings
- Customers stay on the cloud provider’s contract and brand
- Percona is the scaling and operations backbone behind those offerings (not a separate customer face)

**Customer gets:** Cloud-branded MySQL and PostgreSQL services powered by Percona expertise, under the cloud contract they already have.

### Value-added resellers (VARs)

**Who:** Resellers that already sell hardware and software to a set account base (for example SHI, Insight, Paragon, QBS, CanCom).

**How we work together**

- VARs are strong on product SKUs. Database **services** are harder for many of their teams
- Percona helps VAR teams sell consulting, projects, and ongoing support as clear line items
- Percona sells services and expertise, not proprietary database licenses, so partner pricing is **project- and services-based**, not classic license SKU sheets
- Clear services packages make those deals easier to close

**Customer gets:** A familiar reseller plus database experts, with services priced in a way that matches how the work is sold.

## Why partner with Percona

- Help customers spend less on databases and platforms without buying a closed platform they do not need
- Keep customer choice open with open source and source-available databases they can move across vendors and environments
- Support work on-prem, in hybrid estates, and across clouds, including Kubernetes and OpenShift when that is the path
- Bring architecture, migration, and production guidance into the customer conversation early
- Add Expert Support, Expert Consulting, and services when the work needs hands-on Percona people
- Help partner-led deals move faster, with room to match unique customer needs
- Make buying easier with clear services-based terms partners can take to market
