# Partners

Percona helps partners cut customer database cost, keep customers free of vendor lock-in, and run open source databases on-prem, in hybrid estates, or across clouds.

## How we partner

Percona partners with companies that help customers decide what databases should cost, who controls them, and where they run. The work combines the partner’s customer context with Percona database software and engineers who run those engines in production.

Engagements usually start with consulting, professional services, and migration help. When a customer needs day-to-day operational coverage, such as backups, high availability, upgrades, or on-call response their team cannot carry alone, the relationship can expand into longer operational support.

## Why partner with Percona

- Help customers lower database and platform spend without buying a closed platform they do not need
- Keep customer choice open with open source and source-available databases they can move across vendors and environments
- Support workloads on-prem, in hybrid estates, and across clouds, including Kubernetes and OpenShift when that is the path
- Bring architecture, migration, and production guidance into the customer conversation early
- Add Expert Support, Expert Consulting, and services when the work needs hands-on Percona people
