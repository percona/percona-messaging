# Partners

This directory holds canonical messaging for Percona’s Partners home: how we partner, who we partner with, and the path to partner interest. Primary CTA is partner interest and Partnerships triage. This is not a priced offer, SKU, or sales-demo form.

v1 is a thin hub only. Hub frame only: no partner-type taxonomy on the page. Vertical and ICP language stays internal. Featured-partner slots (including Red Hat) stay out of the hub until Partnerships clears adding them.

Service packaging stays under `offerings/`. Product language stays under `products/`.

## Audience

- Prospective and existing partners evaluating how to work with Percona
- Field, Partnerships, and web teams adapting approved hub language

## Lead vs defer

| Lead on the hub | Defer or secondary | Keep off the hub story |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cost, lock-in, deliver anywhere | Full product or services catalog | Formal Red Hat alliance, co-sell, MDF, or deal registration unless Partnerships confirms |
| Consultative expert posture | Managed services / ExpertOps as the hero | HexaCluster / PACE as the hub narrative |
| Consulting, professional services, migration | Priced SKUs, bundles, marketplace portal copy | IBM → Red Hat path; unsigned partners |
| Hub frame only (no taxonomy labels) | Partner-type taxonomy, program tiers, designations | Vertical or ICP words on the page |
| CTA: Partner with us → Partnerships | Featured partner logos and named-partner blocks | Invented partners, win metrics, "signed partnership" language |
| — | Standard sales lead / demo form as primary CTA | Multidimensional-partner examples that are not logo-cleared |
