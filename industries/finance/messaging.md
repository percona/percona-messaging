# Percona for Finance

## Percona for Finance {#percona-for-finance}

Financial services teams run databases through payments, reporting peaks, and audits. Percona supports those estates on infrastructure the customer operates, with Percona Expert Support, Percona ExpertOps, and Percona Expert Consulting and Services.

Coverage includes MySQL®, MariaDB Server, PostgreSQL, MongoDB®-compatible environments, Valkey, and Redis. Percona develops and maintains Percona Server for MySQL, Percona Server for PostgreSQL, and Percona Server for MongoDB (source available). Percona supports MariaDB Server, Valkey, and Redis users with operational expertise.

### Payments, audits, and peaks

- **Payments and card data:** Card-data environments need auditable encryption, key management, and access controls that teams can demonstrate. The payments and PCI-DSS use case lives in [Security, sovereignty, and compliance](/use-cases-value-pillars/security-sovereignty-compliance.md).
- **Audit evidence and key custody:** Reviewers expect proof of who accessed data, how identity is enforced, and where keys are held. Audit, access, and key custody controls are described in [Security, sovereignty, and compliance](/use-cases-value-pillars/security-sovereignty-compliance.md).
- **Reporting peaks and change freezes:** Tax season, quarter close, and financial audit windows raise load and change risk together. Scoped consulting for performance readiness, backup and restore readiness, and migration or upgrade alignment with those windows is in [Solution bundles](/offerings/solution-bundles/messaging.md).
- **Availability through the peak:** Backup health, failover, and observability still have to hold while reporting load is high. Continuity and recovery scenarios are in [Performance and reliability](/use-cases-value-pillars/performance-reliability.md).
