# Percona for Financial Services

For financial services organizations running business-critical databases, Percona helps protect service availability, strengthen data security, and control database costs with open source software and expert services.

Banks, payment providers, fintechs, and investment firms must modernize while maintaining the systems customers depend on, and the cost of getting it wrong is rising. A single high-impact outage now costs a financial services firm an average of [$1.8 million per hour](https://newrelic.com/press-release/20260120). Between January 2023 and February 2025, nine major UK banks and building societies logged more than 800 hours of unplanned IT outages. Up to [70% of bank IT spending](https://www.accenture.com/en/insights/banking/accenture-banking-trends-2026) goes to keeping existing systems running and meeting regulatory demands, leaving little for new work. Their database teams need to support reliable transaction processing, demonstrate security controls, recover services within agreed limits, and manage growing infrastructure demands.

Percona brings together expertise across MySQL®, PostgreSQL, and MongoDB® environments, with deployment options across on-premises, cloud, hybrid, and Kubernetes infrastructure. Percona Monitoring and Management (PMM) provides database and query performance visibility. Database software and supporting tools provide capabilities for availability, backup, recovery, and security appropriate to each technology.

Institutions can combine these capabilities with 24×7 Expert Support for troubleshooting and advice, ExpertOps for ongoing hands-on database management, and Expert Consulting and Services for migrations, architecture, performance, and security projects. This helps teams strengthen database operations while retaining control over their technology and deployment choices.

## Open source for the demands of regulated financial services

Percona combines the transparency and choice of open source with database security capabilities, tested software components, and specialist support. Financial institutions can build and operate databases around their requirements for availability, recovery, access control, and audit evidence while retaining greater control over deployment and provider choices. Percona brings the expertise to assess, implement, and maintain those capabilities for critical financial workloads.

## Customer Challenges and Value Alignment

### Performance and Operational Resilience

- **Keep payments and customer account services responsive:** Database slowdowns can delay transactions and disrupt access to financial services before a database becomes unavailable. Downtime now costs Global 2000 financial services firms an average of [$309 million](https://www.splunk.com/en_us/form/the-hidden-costs-of-downtime-in-financial-services.html) a year, double the 2024 figure. PMM provides database dashboards and Query Analytics across MySQL, PostgreSQL, and MongoDB, helping teams investigate expensive queries and resource bottlenecks. This gives database and application teams a clearer basis for addressing the problems affecting service performance.
- **Support availability requirements for critical financial workloads:** Payments, trading, and banking applications require database architectures that account for failures and planned maintenance. Percona helps teams assess appropriate availability approaches. [Merchant Warrior](https://www.percona.com/customer-story/merchant-warrior-counts-on-percona-for-critical-availability/), which processes millions of transactions per month, uses Percona XtraDB Cluster to support database availability and resilience and monitoring through PMM, giving the team access to DB expertise and visibility into health and performance. The architecture and failover procedures must be configured and tested against the institution's requirements.
- **Plan recovery around acceptable service interruption and data loss:** Financial institutions need to establish whether databases can be restored within the limits set for the services they support. Percona's backup and recovery tools include pgBackRest for PostgreSQL and Percona Backup for MongoDB, with point-in-time recovery available in supported configurations. Recovery planning and testing should establish how database restoration supports the return of the financial application to service.
- **Bring specialist expertise into incidents affecting financial services:** When database problems interrupt transactions or customer access, internal teams need an established escalation route. Percona Expert Support provides 24×7 troubleshooting and advisory assistance. ExpertOps adds ongoing monitoring, maintenance, and hands-on database operations for institutions requiring operational coverage. Responsibilities and response commitments depend on the selected agreement.

**Customer proof:** [Merchant Warrior](https://www.percona.com/customer-story/merchant-warrior-counts-on-percona-for-critical-availability/) supports more than 30,000 customers and processes millions of transactions per month. Its published story describes using Percona support, Percona XtraDB Cluster, and PMM for database availability, resilience, and performance visibility.

### Security, Auditability, and Data Control

- **Protect sensitive financial data:** Financial institutions need security controls that fit their workloads and deployment environments. Percona helps teams implement database encryption, access controls, audit logging, and security maintenance using capabilities appropriate to each database technology.
- **Support compliance with operational evidence:** Security and compliance teams need visibility into how databases are configured, accessed, maintained, and recovered. Percona's database software and expertise help teams establish and assess database controls supporting their obligations under frameworks such as PCI DSS and regulations such as GDPR and DORA. Compliance depends on the institution's wider implementation and processes.
- **Retain control over data placement:** Data residency requirements and internal risk policies can constrain infrastructure choices. Percona supports database operations across on-premises, cloud, and hybrid environments, helping institutions select deployment locations that fit their requirements while preserving flexibility.

### Cost Efficiency and Vendor Independence

- **Reduce exposure to proprietary database costs:** Licensing renewals and commercial restrictions can limit how financial institutions scale and modernize. Percona offers open source database alternatives and migration expertise to help teams evaluate options, reduce licensing expenditure, and retain more control over their database strategy.
- **Address the full cost of database operations:** License savings are only part of the business case. Infrastructure, migration, maintenance, specialist staffing, and incident response also affect total cost. Percona combines monitoring, performance tuning, automation, and expert services to help teams identify inefficient resource use and assess the economics of their database estate.
- **Preserve future choices:** A database decision should leave room for changes in hosting, support providers, and business requirements. Percona helps teams evaluate migration and deployment options with attention to compatibility, operational dependencies, and exit requirements.

**Customer proof:** [BBVA](https://www.percona.com/customer-story/bbva-migrates-document-oriented-database-nosql-workloads-to-percona-avoiding-license-costs-and-lock-in/) migrated 35 TB across more than 400 servers from MongoDB Enterprise to Percona Server for MongoDB (source available). This move eliminated the associated licensing costs and improved backup recovery time by 20%.

### Modernization and Emerging Workloads

- **Modernize with a defined migration path:** Moving critical databases requires clarity on application compatibility, data migration, validation, and cutover. Percona's consulting and migration services help teams assess those requirements and plan transitions around business constraints and acceptable downtime.
- **Reduce operational complexity across environments:** Financial institutions often operate several database technologies across established infrastructure and newer cloud platforms. Percona Monitoring and Management provides database and query performance visibility, while Percona Operators automate supported database operations in Kubernetes. Expert services help teams apply these tools within their operating model.
- **Prepare the database layer for AI applications:** New AI applications introduce additional requirements for data access, performance, and security. Percona Distribution for PostgreSQL includes pgvector for vector search and embeddings, helping teams evaluate workloads that can use their existing PostgreSQL platform. Database architecture, access controls, and capacity still need to be assessed for each application.
